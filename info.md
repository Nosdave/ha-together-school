Track your child's **school bus** in Home Assistant: live position on the map,
check-in / check-out, the scheduled pickup and arrival, and a flag when the
school reports a missed bus.

Works with any school on the Together School platform. At setup you enter the
**school code** (the subdomain of your school's Together School site), the
**mobile number** registered in your child's profile (not the email address)
and your password. The password is exchanged for an access token during setup
and is **not stored**.

One device per child, discovered automatically from your parent account.
